Hydrogen Production Plant

Design of a Hydrogen Production Plant with Integrated Biochar Carbon Capture System to Produce 10,000 m³/hr of Hydrogen via Steam Methane Reforming (SMR).

Explore Project Back to Projects

Project Overview

This final-year Chemical Engineering design project focused on the development of a hydrogen production plant capable of producing 10,000 m³/hr of hydrogen from natural gas using Steam Methane Reforming (SMR), integrated with a biochar-based carbon capture system.

The project addressed the environmental challenge associated with conventional SMR hydrogen production by integrating biochar as a CO₂ adsorbent.

The design covered process development, material and energy balances, equipment selection, process control, environmental considerations, and economic evaluation.

Process Design

The process design integrates several major process units required for hydrogen production and carbon capture.

  • Feed preheating and preparation
  • Hydrogenation and natural gas desulfurization
  • Pre-reforming
  • Steam Methane Reforming
  • High-Temperature Shift Conversion
  • Hydrogen purification using Pressure Swing Adsorption (PSA)
  • Biochar-based CO₂ adsorption and capture
  • Heat integration and energy recovery

In the reformer, methane reacts with steam at approximately 850–900 °C and 28 atm to produce hydrogen-rich synthesis gas.

The gas then undergoes shift conversion before hydrogen purification through Pressure Swing Adsorption (PSA).

The separated CO₂-rich stream is directed to the biochar adsorption section for carbon capture.

Biochar Carbon Capture Integration

A key feature of the project was the integration of biochar as a CO₂ adsorbent.

The design investigated biochar as a sustainable alternative for reducing emissions associated with conventional Steam Methane Reforming hydrogen production.

The project also considered the potential production of biochar from Nigerian agricultural residues such as corn cobs and rice husks.

This creates an opportunity to connect carbon management with agricultural waste utilisation while supporting more sustainable hydrogen production.
