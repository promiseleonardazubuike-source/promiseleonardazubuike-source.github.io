Kinetics and Optimisation of Enzymatic Hydrolysis of Starch from Corn Cob

Bachelor of Engineering Research Project

Download PDF Back to Portfolio

Project Overview

This research investigated the enzymatic hydrolysis of starch extracted from corn cob using α-amylase. Response Surface Methodology (RSM) was employed to optimise enzyme concentration, pH, and reaction time in order to maximise glucose yield while developing kinetic models to describe the reaction behaviour.

Research Objectives

  • Recover starch from corn cob.
  • Hydrolyse starch using α-amylase.
  • Optimise pH, enzyme concentration and reaction time.
  • Develop kinetic models.
  • Validate optimum operating conditions.
